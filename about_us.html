A Trusted Name in Steel Construction

Our journey began with a simple mission: to blend cutting-edge technology with local expertise to create a world-class recycling hub. Today, we employ a skilled team of over 500 professionals who prioritize safety, innovation, and community welfare. From our strategic location in Bangladesh, we serve global clients by transforming ships into reusable steel, components, and other materials, contributing to a circular economy.
