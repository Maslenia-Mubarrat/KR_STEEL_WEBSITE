About Us

At KR Steel Limited, we're more than a ship recycling facility—we're a catalyst for sustainable industry change. Established in Bangladesh, our operations align with the Hong Kong Convention, which mandates strict guidelines for safe ship recycling to prevent pollution, protect workers, and promote resource efficiency. This compliance ensures that every vessel we handle is dismantled with precision, minimizing environmental harm and maximizing material reuse.
